Yippo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Yippo is a family chore app. Parents create chores for their children, children check them off, and parents approve them. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it.

The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work. We show no ads, do no tracking, use no analytics tools, and never sell or share personal data for marketing. A parent can delete the whole family's data at any time from inside the app.

Who uses Yippo

  • Parents create an account with an email address and password.
  • Children never create an account and never give an email address or phone number. A parent creates the child's profile and connects the child's device with a one-time code or QR code. Children can only use Yippo once a parent has set them up.

What we collect

From parents

  • Email address and password (the password is stored only in hashed form).
  • The parent's display name.

About children (entered by the parent, or by the child in the app)

  • First name, age and a chosen animal "buddy".
  • An optional selfie, if the child or parent adds one. Parents can remove it at any time.
  • Chores, completion history, points, levels, rewards requested and daily challenge answers.
  • Photos of finished chores, but only when the parent turns on "photo proof" for that chore.

From devices

  • A push notification token, so a child's device can be told when a parent asks for a chore to be done again.
  • An anonymous sign-in identifier for each child's device.

We do not collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers or the child's photo library. The camera is used only when someone chooses to take a photo.

Who can see the data

  • Photos, selfies and activity are visible only to the parents and children in the same family.
  • Yippo has no public profiles, chat or social features.

Services we use to run Yippo

  • Supabase stores the app's data and photos on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech turns chore titles and descriptions into the natural voice that reads chores aloud. Only the chore text is sent: no names, photos or account details. The resulting audio is stored on our servers.
  • Expo push service and Apple Push Notification service deliver notifications to children's devices. They receive the device's push token and the notification text, for example the chore title and any note the parent wrote.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep data

We keep a family's data while their account exists. When a parent deletes the account (the Delete account button on the parent home screen), we permanently delete the family's chores, history, photos, selfies and every parent and child login. Voice clips made from that family's custom chores are deleted as well.

A parent can also remove a child's selfie at any time, and can delete individual chores and rewards.

Children's privacy

Yippo is designed for families with children aged about 5–12. Children use Yippo under a parent's account, set up and controlled by the parent. We collect only what the app needs to work, and parents can review the data in the app and delete it at any time. We follow the principles of the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020).

Your rights

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell parents in the app.
